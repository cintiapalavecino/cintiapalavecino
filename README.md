# Hi, I'm Cintia Palavecino 👋

**Eco-epidemiologist and data scientist** working on zoonoses and vector-borne diseases in Argentina.
I apply statistical modelling in R to understand how climate, environment, and host factors shape the dynamics of vector-borne and zoonotic diseases.

- 🦟 Postdoctoral fellow at **CONICET / CEVARCAM (Facultad de Ingeniería y Ciencias Hídricas, Universidad Nacional del Litoral)**, developing tools for the integrated surveillance of *Aedes aegypti* and dengue in collaboration with local governments in Santa Fe, Argentina
- 🌡️ Modelling how **climate and environmental conditions** shape vector dynamics in space and time, and supporting **entomo-virological surveillance** for early detection of viral circulation
- 🧬 PhD in Veterinary Sciences (UNL): host–parasite ecology, co-infections and eco-immunology
- 🎓 Teaching biochemistry (UADER) and mentoring students and fellows in statistical modelling with R

## 🔧 Methods and tools

**Statistical modelling:** GLMM / LMM (`lme4`, `glmmTMB`), GAM and quantile GAM (`mgcv`, `qgam`),
distributed lag non-linear models (`dlnm`), model selection and averaging (`MuMIn`),
residual diagnostics (`DHARMa`, `performance`), multivariate GLMs (`mvabund`), PLS regression

**Spatial analysis:** `sf`, `terra`, `spdep`, `spatialreg`, `tmap`,
global and local Moran's I, bivariate LISA, spatial lag (SAR) models, remote-sensing LST data

**Data and reporting:** `tidyverse`, `ggplot2`, R Markdown

## 📂 Featured repositories

| Repository | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **aedes-climate-anomalies** | Weekly climate anomalies vs. a 1991–2020 baseline, GAMs and distributed lag non-linear models (DLNM) linking temperature anomalies with *Ae. aegypti* oviposition across epidemic and non-epidemic dengue seasons |
| **aedes-surveillance-reconquista** | Comparing ovitraps and gravid traps over time (negative binomial GAMs with effort offsets, lagged egg-to-female effects; poster, II Argentine Congress of Statistics 2026) and trap positivity maps produced for municipal surveillance reports |
| **coinfection-tolerance-models** | Tolerance to *Trypanosoma cruzi* and *Trichinella spiralis* co-infection under food restriction and social conflict, using linear and Gamma mixed models, Benjamini–Hochberg correction and `DHARMa` diagnostics |
